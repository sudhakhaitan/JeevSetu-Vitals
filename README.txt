JeevSetu AI — Musculoskeletal Examination
Standalone prototype ZIP for GitHub Pages or local browser testing.

Files:
- index.html: self-contained examination module (no external libraries required)
- README.txt: this file

Included:
- Immediate warning screen that locks routine examination when a warning is selected
- Musculoskeletal symptom screening corresponding to 18.7
- General observation and detailed 19.10 examination pathways
- Multiple joint/region records, side-specific muscle strength records, spine region records
- Senior functional assessment and bone-health risk screening
- Local browser draft save/restore
- Browser print function

No patient demographic entry, no clinical-note generation, no AI diagnosis, and no treatment recommendation.

Safety note: prototype for trained examiners; clinical judgment and local referral protocols apply. Do not force movement in suspected injury or severe pain.

Update: Pain severity is recorded separately in each joint/region examination card (0–10), rather than once in the initial symptom screening. This allows different pain scores for multiple affected joints. Spine regions also have their own pain score.
