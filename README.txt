JeevSetu — Integrated Vitals, General Examination & Lymph Node Examination

This build adds a lymph-node examination section to the integrated vitals/general examination form. Findings are included in the consolidated clinical note generated by the existing Generate Consolidated Clinical Note button and in Print / Save PDF.

Test: Extract the ZIP and open index.html in Chrome. Enter data, generate the consolidated note, and print/save PDF. Check ABI, BMI and WHR calculations.

Clinical safety: This is a recording aid, not a diagnostic tool. Normal accessible-node findings do not exclude deep-node disease, tuberculosis or malignancy. Review findings with a qualified clinician before field deployment.
