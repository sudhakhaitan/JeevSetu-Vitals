Symptoms screened: headache; dizziness/vertigo; fainting/near-fainting; weakness; numbness/tingling; speech/language difficulty; visual neurological symptoms; balance/walking difficulty; falls; memory/cognitive change; tremor/abnormal movement; seizure/loss of consciousness. Present symptoms can be described in the details field.

Safety: If any immediate warning sign is marked Yes, routine symptom screening and examination are stopped and the urgent referral warning is displayed. This prototype does not diagnose and requires clinician review and field testing before patient-care deployment.
