- Endocrine, Urinary, Male Genital, Female Genital & Breast, ENT, Eyes & Vision, Dental / Oral, Skin, Vascular.

Use the same browser and device. Complete the Vitals → Musculoskeletal module and generate/print its consolidated clinical note within that module.
