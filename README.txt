JeevSetu — 19.18 Female Genital Examination
GitHub Pages ready standalone module.
Combined female genital + breast examination module. Includes indication/consent/privacy, female genital examination where indicated, bilateral breast inspection and lump assessment with digital breast map, nipple examination, axillary/supraclavicular nodes, detailed clinician note, and abnormal-findings-only clinical summary.
Urinary examination is not duplicated here. Internal vaginal/cervical examination is not assumed and is only performed when clinically indicated, appropriately consented, and within examiner scope.
AI does not diagnose malignancy from examination alone.


Revision: patient-reported vaginal discharge is now recorded separately from discharge seen at examination; breast lump assessment supports multiple lumps per breast with separate details and locations; an Additional Findings field is placed at the end of the form and included in the clinician note.
