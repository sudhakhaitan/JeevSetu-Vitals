19.18 — FEMALE GENITAL EXAMINATION

Female genital examination is performed only where clinically indicated and with patient consent, privacy and appropriate chaperone arrangements. Breast examination remains part of this combined female examination module.

Internal vaginal/cervical examination is not assumed in this section; perform only when clinically indicated, appropriately consented and within the examiner’s scope.
