Dedicated examiner module · Diabetes and endocrine screening · Detailed thyroid examination · No patient demographics repeated

How to use

Complete the immediate warning screen first. Record observed findings and patient-reported symptoms; do not infer a diagnosis from a single finding. Use “Not examined” when an item was not assessed. This prototype does not diagnose or recommend treatment. Diabetic Foot Examination is intentionally a separate module and is not included here.
