How to use

Record examination findings only within your training and scope. Examine both sides where relevant. Do not force painful movements. If a warning sign is present, stop routine examination and arrange appropriate clinical assessment. This tool does not diagnose or recommend treatment.

4. Joint-by-joint examination

Add one record for every affected joint or region. Multiple joints and both sides can be recorded independently. Record pain severity separately for each joint/region, because different sites may have different pain scores (0 = no pain, 10 = worst imaginable pain).

5. Spine examination

Do not force movement if trauma, severe pain or another contraindication is suspected.

6. Muscle examination

MRC power scale: 0 = no contraction; 1 = trace contraction; 2 = movement with gravity eliminated; 3 = movement against gravity; 4 = movement against resistance but weaker than normal; 5 = normal power. Record only if trained to assess.
