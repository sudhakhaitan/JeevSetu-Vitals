19.19 — MALE GENITAL EXAMINATION

Not routine for every male patient.

External genital examination is performed only where clinically indicated and with appropriate privacy and patient permission. Prostate examination is a separate optional component within this section and is performed only when clinically indicated and the patient agrees.

A. External examination — where indicated

Penis / meatus

Scrotum

Right testis

Left testis

Right epididymis

Left epididymis

Hernia

B. Prostate Examination — Digital Rectal Examination (DRE)

DRE is optional. Do not perform it routinely. Perform only when clinically indicated, appropriate, and the patient has agreed. If it is not required or the patient does not permit it, select the appropriate status and do not perform the examination.

C. Urgent findings

Urgent findings: Acute urinary retention, significant haematuria, acute severe testicular pain/swelling, suspected torsion, or severe infection/systemic illness → immediate clinical attention.
AI safety: AI must not equate prostate enlargement with cancer.
